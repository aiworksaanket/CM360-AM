Why do we need different attribution models?

01

Make better optimization decisions

Use the insights to improve future campaigns.

02

Measure marketing impact

See what marketing activity contributed to the conversion.

03

Compare channel contribution

See how different channels contributed to the conversion.

04

One rule cannot show every perspective

One attribution model cannot give us a complete picture of the customer journey.

05

Customers take different paths

A conversion can happen after different combinations of touchpoints.

06

Different models answer different questions

One model may focus on the first touch, another on the last, or share credit across touchpoints.

16 / 17

We understand the models —
but which one is used in reporting?

All models help us look at the same customer journey from different perspectives.
Here’s how they are used in CM360.

For reporting

Floodlight Attribution Model

Standard CM360 reports use the Floodlight attribution model to assign conversion credit.

Floodlight Conversion (on advertiser’s website)
→
CM360 Reporting
→
Credit Assigned (Floodlight Model)

Last eligible click gets 100% credit.

If there is no eligible click, last eligible impression gets 100% credit.

For analysis

Other Attribution Models

Used in the Attribution Modeling Tool to understand how conversion credit could be distributed across different touchpoints in the same customer journey.

These models help us understand how other touchpoints contribute and how credit could be assigned differently for the same conversion.
